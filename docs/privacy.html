Privacy Policy – Wait 24h

No data leaves your device.

Wait 24h stores everything (your settings, pending reminders, savings totals and

donation state) in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local).

  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts.
  • No server is contacted by the extension. It makes no network requests of its own.
  • Product titles, prices and page URLs are read on supported shops only to show the pause overlay and to save a reminder, and are kept only on your device.
  • Donation links open in a normal browser tab, at your request. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on their own page; the extension never sees payment details.
  • The optional thank-you page only lets the extension mark you as a supporter locally (a flag stored on your device).

You can erase all data at any time by removing the extension.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
