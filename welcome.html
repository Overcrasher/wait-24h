Welcome to Wait 24h

A pause before impulse buys, and a tally of the money you kept.

Try it now

  1. 1Open a product page on a supported shop, such as Amazon, eMAG or AliExpress.
  2. 2Click the buy button. Wait 24h steps in and offers to remind you tomorrow.
  3. 3Tomorrow, decide: still want it, or skip it. Skipped items add up to your savings.

Good to know

Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Pin it: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Wait 24h.
